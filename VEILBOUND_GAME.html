THE ETERNAL ECLIPSE • SOLO BATTLE • COLLECTION ALPHA 0.6
YOU: Crimson Court · AI: Thorn Coven
Solo mode: You play Crimson Court against a Thorn Coven computer opponent. Select a card, tap Play, and tap targets when prompted. Tap a creature then Attack; the AI automatically decides whether to block. The AI summons creatures, uses spells and relics, places Hex counters, attacks and chooses blockers. Both decks contain 40 cards. Each player begins at 30 vitality. The shared Eclipse advances at the start of each Crimson Court turn, beginning with Blood Moon. Essence grows by one each personal turn, to 10. The first player skips their first draw. Alpha limitations: The AI uses tactical heuristics rather than learning, some complex rules interactions remain simplified; illustrated artwork is shared among several cards, and no cloud saves, multiplayer or in-app purchases are included.
